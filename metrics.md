## How we count

- **Attendee** = checked in at the event (in-person) or joined the call for ≥15 min (virtual).
- **Unique attendee** = distinct person across all events, de-duplicated by email.
- **Contributor onboarded** = someone whose first OSS PR (any repo) happened within 30 days of a GDG JIS-U event they attended.

These definitions are deliberately conservative — we'd rather under-count than overstate.
