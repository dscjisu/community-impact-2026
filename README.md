# GDG on Campus — JIS University (2025–26)

> *A year of building, learning, and growing together.*

This repository is a memorial of everything the **Google Developer Group on Campus, JIS University** accomplished during the **2025–26 term**, led by **[Ayushman Bhattacharya](https://github.com/Circuit-Overtime)** as Chapter Organizer.

What started as a small community of curious developers became a full-fledged movement — one that brought students, mentors, speakers, and sponsors together across campuses, time zones, and disciplines.

---

## The Journey

### Codesprint 2.0 — Where It All Kicked Off
**August 15–16, 2025**

A two-day hackathon that set the tone for the year. Students came together to build, break, and ship — some for the very first time. It wasn't about winning. It was about showing up.

### Building the Core Team — September 2025

Twenty people were handpicked through portfolio reviews and interviews to form the backbone of the chapter. Designers, writers, mentors, and communicators — each chosen not just for skill, but for heart.

### Google Study Jams — Unlocking the Cloud

The chapter rallied **87+ participants** through Google Cloud's Study Jams program — completing labs, earning badges, and pushing toward **Tier 1 status** with 100 completions. Hundreds of free Google Cloud credits were distributed, and for many students, it was their first real exposure to cloud computing.

### Hacktoberfest x Study Jams Session — October 13, 2025

A cross-campus collaboration hosted at JIS University, bringing together leads from **GDG NiT** and **GDG GNIT** for a morning of open-source learning and hands-on contribution.

### Hacktoberfest Meetup 2025 — The Big One
**October 27, 2025 — JIS University, Room 1109**

This was the chapter's flagship event of the year — a full-day gathering from morning till evening, featuring:

- **Speakers from across the industry** — from vCluster platform advocates to GitHub Campus Experts, full-stack developers, and the founders of Pollinations.ai joining virtually from across the world
- **A felicitation ceremony** honoring the seniors who built the chapter before us — Manish Gupta, Shemanti Pal, Rahul Kamliya, and Ankita Chakraborty
- **Faculty support** from professors and the HOD of CSE who believed in what we were doing
- **Sponsorships** from ISTE and Pollinations.ai that made it all possible — and core team members who quietly funded gaps from their own pockets

**Notable Speakers:**
Hrittik Roy (vCluster) · Abhisekh Kushwaha (GitHub Campus Expert) · Debojeet Karmakar (Prepverse) · Samrat Talukdar (GDG NiT) · Nazreen Imam (GDG GNIT) · Saugata Sarkar (Hyland) · Sawan (Keyshade) · Thomash Haferlach & Elliot Fouchy (Pollinations.ai)

**Early 2026**

The final major event of the term. Teams competed, judges deliberated, and three teams stood out:

| Rank | Team |
|---|---|

| 1st Runners-Up | The Build Guild |

Detailed feedback was given to the top five teams — because this chapter always believed in building people up, not just picking winners.

## A Note From the Chapter

We weren't the biggest GDG chapter. We weren't always the most polished. But we showed up — every single time. We emailed at midnight, designed banners at dawn, ran around campus with flex prints, and somehow always pulled it off.

This repository exists so that the next team knows what happened, how it happened, and that it's absolutely possible to do it again — and better.

To whoever leads next: the community is yours now. Take care of it.
